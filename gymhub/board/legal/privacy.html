Privacy policy

Last updated {{updated}}

Human Gym is a practice board for programming exercises. This board is run by {{operator}} ("we"). This page says what we collect when you use it, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and how you can get a copy or delete it. Questions go to {{contact}}.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, your name, your profile photo's address and a Google account id. We do not get your password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • What you do on the board. The code you save and submit, the output of running it (test results, errors, benchmark times), your progress through each gym, hints you opened, answers to questions, your spaced-review schedule, and the gyms you create, edit or are invited to.
  • Settings and tokens. Your notification settings, and the names of any API tokens or machines you add (we keep only a hash of each token's secret).
  • Usage counts. How many jobs you ran and for how long, and how much of the AI tutor you used, so we can apply fair-use limits.
  • Technical logs. Like most web services, our hosting records requests (time, page, IP address, browser) and errors. We use these to keep the board running and secure.

We do not use advertising or third-party analytics, and we do not sell your data. The board sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in.

Why we use it

  • To run the service you asked for: sign you in, grade your code, save your progress and show it back to you. (Legal basis in the EU/UK: performing our agreement with you.)
  • To keep the board fair and safe: rate limits, quotas, preventing abuse, fixing bugs. (Legitimate interests.)
  • To meet legal obligations, if any apply.

Who else sees it

  • Other people on the board see only what the board shows them: the owner and editors of a gym you were invited to see your email on its sharing list; gyms with a leaderboard show your name and best time to that gym's learners; gym editors see totals (how many people solved an exercise), never who. Board admins can see the list of members (name, email, role, usage).
  • Machines that grade code. Your submissions run on machines connected to the board, which may be owned by the board's admins or by other members who added a machine. The machine receives your code and the exercise, not your email or name.
  • Service providers who process data for us: Google Cloud (hosting, database, file storage, logs, and sign-in), and Anthropic, only when you use the AI tutor: it receives the exercise, your code and its output, not your name or email.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their standard data-protection terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and your work until you delete your account. Scratch runs are deleted within a day, saves and hints after 30 days, rate-limit counters within days. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited time (typically 30 days).

Your choices and rights

  • Get a copy. On Your data you can download everything the board holds about you as a JSON file.
  • Delete your account. On the same page you can delete your account. This removes your profile, progress, saved code, submissions, tokens and machines straight away, and deletes the gyms you own for everyone. Copies in backups and logs disappear when those expire.
  • You can also ask us at {{contact}} to correct your data, or object to or restrict how we use it. If you are in the EU or UK you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

The board is not meant for children under 16. If you believe a child has signed up, tell us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell members on the board before a change that matters takes effect.
