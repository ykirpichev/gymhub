# How this gym works

A lesson is a page to read. It has no grader: **Mark as read** completes it, and it does not count as an exercise.

> [!NOTE]
> Statements, hints and choices can use math: inline as $O(\log n)$, or on a line of its own:
>
> $$\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i$$

> [!TIP]
> Every other exercise here is a different kind: code, a typed answer, choices, putting steps in order, matching pairs, filling in blanks, an essay and a checklist.
